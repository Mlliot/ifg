About IFG

Providing specialty insurance since 1985.

IFG Companies is one of the largest privately held insurance groups in the United States. Relying on strong producer relationships and a knowledgeable team of underwriters. IFG Companies provide property-casualty insurance on both an admitted and non-admitted basis.

A forward-thinking company, IFG Companies is focused on the changes in the industry and technology. Investing in the future ensures IFG Companies continued success within a constantly evolving marketplace.
